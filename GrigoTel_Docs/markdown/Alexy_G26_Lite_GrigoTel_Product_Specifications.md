Product Specifications: Alexy G26 Lite

Status: Internal/Confidential

1. Executive Summary

The Alexy G26 Lite is designed as a lighter, more accessible take on a
premium smartphone, combining solid performance with a clean, simple

design. It aims to compete with the iPhone lineup while focusing on open

ecosystem support, flexible hardware, and strong, future-ready security.

3. Environmental Resistance

Designed for everyday use, the Alexy G26 Lite does not include water or dust resistance.
No Ingress Protection supported; The device is not certified against dust or water
exposure, so it should be kept away from moisture, liquids, and dusty environments to
maintain optimal performance.
