Corporate Profile

1. Company Overview

GrigoTel is a French-based multinational technology corporation specializing
in advanced telecommunications, consumer electronics, and neural-link
software. Founded with the mission to "Restore Hardware Sovereignty,"

GrigoTel challenges the current mobile duopoly by offering high-
performance, open-ecosystem devices that prioritize user privacy and
modular longevity.

3. Core Values

  Modular Excellence: Designing hardware that evolves with the user

(e.g., the Halo Lens system and expandable storage).

  Quantum Privacy: Implementation of quantum-safe encryption

standards as a baseline, not a premium feature.

  European Engineering: Leveraging French design aesthetics and

rigorous EU data protection standards.

  Sustainability: Commitment to 95% recycled titanium and ethically

sourced minerals across all production lines.

4. Operational Hubs

While headquartered in France, GrigoTel operates a decentralized R&D
network to tap into global talent:

  Paris, France: Global HQ, Industrial Design & Software Engineering.
  Berlin, Germany: Security Systems & Encryption Lab.
  Tokyo, Japan: Optical Engineering (Halo Lens Partnership).
  Shenzhen, China: Supply Chain & Logistics Management.
