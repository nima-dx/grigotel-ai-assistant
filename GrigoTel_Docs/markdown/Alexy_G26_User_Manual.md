4. Battery Management

The Alexy G26 is designed with a Sustainability focus, featuring a user-replaceable
battery and a 93% recycled titanium chassis. Follow these steps to safely maintain or
repair your device.

4.1. Preparation and Safety



Power Down: Completely shut down the Alexy G26 before beginning any repair
to protect the Helios G1M Neural Chip and internal circuitry.

  Environment: Work on a flat, anti-static surface. While the phone has an IP69

Rating and Hydro-Guard nanocoating, repairs should always be performed in a
dry environment to prevent moisture from entering the internal seal during the
process.

4.2. Battery Replacement Process

Unlike traditional sealed flagships, the G26 is engineered for hardware accessibility.

1.  Chassis Access: Locate the recessed security toggles on the titanium chassis.
2.  Disengage Seals: Carefully release the rear panel. Note that the Hydro-Guard
Internal Seal provides a proprietary nanocoating layer that may offer slight
resistance; do not force the panel.

3.  Remove Old Battery: Disconnect the localized power ribbon from the 5,500

mAh battery unit.

4.  Insert New Unit: Align the new high-capacity cell. The Adaptive Hardware
system will automatically calibrate the new battery to maintain the 95/100
Battery Life benchmark.

5.  Reseal: Press the rear panel firmly until the IP69-rated seals click into place,

ensuring the device remains waterproof against high-pressure jets.

4.3. Post-Repair Calibration

  Rapid Charge Test: Connect the device to a 60W Rapid Charger to verify the

connection.

  System Check: Upon rebooting "Fluid" OS, navigate to "Hardware Health" to

ensure the Thermal Efficiency remains at the target 92%.

  Security Sync: Ensure the Quantum-Safe Encryption and Vein-Scan biometric

systems are functioning correctly after the power cycle.
